## Enabling game teams

Much of my professional work in games has happened one layer below the game
itself: building the technology, workflows and engineering practices that let
designers, artists and programmers create and ship ambitious work across
constrained or rapidly changing platforms.

At **Glu Mobile**, I designed and implemented a shader-authoring pipeline that
reconciled competing commercial and community standards across multiple target
platforms. I also researched and built a high-level game-logic solution that
combined established tools with internal technology, giving projects a
maintainable and high-performance way to author dynamic behaviour.

As **Chief Software Architect at Ideaworks3D / Marmalade**, I defined
architecture and design practices across engineering groups in the UK and
Ukraine. The role combined hands-on technical research with mentoring,
estimation and process improvement, helping distributed teams make consistent
decisions as mobile hardware and platform expectations changed quickly.

Earlier, at **Superscape**, I designed and led development of tools used by
multiple production studios creating games for feature phones and the first
generation of smartphones. The recurring problem was the same one I still
enjoy solving: hide platform complexity without hiding the control that expert
creators need.
