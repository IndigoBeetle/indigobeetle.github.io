## Specialist 3D experience applied close to the hardware

My time at **ARM** was relatively short, but it forms an important bridge
between two decades of real-time 3D work at Superscape and the modern mobile GPU
era.

ARM recruited me directly from Superscape for my specialist experience in 3D
graphics. I joined a newly formed media division working with the technology
ARM had acquired with **Falanx**, a Norwegian GPU company whose work became the
foundation of the Mali product line.

As a Principal Engineer in embedded software, I worked on the software stack
needed to make that graphics hardware useful to application and game
developers. My particular focus was **shader compilers**, alongside driver
components controlling the operation of the 3D accelerator.

The work drew on the full depth of my graphics background: understanding the
high-level intent of rendering code, the constraints of a developing hardware
architecture, and the compiler and runtime systems required to translate
between them reliably and efficiently.

### What this work demonstrates

- Shader language and compiler implementation
- GPU drivers and embedded graphics systems
- Low-level performance and hardware-aware engineering
- The transfer of deep 3D knowledge between VR, games and silicon platforms
