## Full-stack application development

An ongoing engagement with **Nourish Contract Catering**, covering the design
and implementation of a full-stack application for families and schools. The
published mobile app gives users access to daily school menus and events,
categorised recipes and Nourish literature.

The work demonstrates end-to-end product delivery: understanding operational
needs, shaping the domain and user experience, selecting an appropriate
architecture, and delivering both client-facing and server-side functionality.
