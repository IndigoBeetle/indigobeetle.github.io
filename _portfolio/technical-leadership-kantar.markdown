## From specialist team to dependable delivery organisation

As Chief Technology Officer for **Kantar Retail Virtual Reality**, I led the
technology and delivery organisation responsible for the next generation of
virtual-reality software for the retail market. The organisation had evolved
from Red Dot Square Solutions, where I had previously worked with Zahir Mazhar.

The group grew from four people to 35, covering the full delivery lifecycle and
working with offshore partners to balance capability and cost. I introduced
agile ways of working while giving the team room to shape the process around
its own needs, made quality assurance part of development rather than a final
gate, and established DevOps practices for faster, more reliable delivery.

The role extended beyond engineering management. I worked with customers and
partners across Kantar and WPP, guided product and technology strategy, and
oversaw the security and compliance work needed to deliver enterprise software
in a corporate environment. I remained close to architecture and implementation
throughout, using technical depth to make strategic decisions concrete.

### Outcomes and responsibilities

- Grew and shaped a multidisciplinary software delivery team
- Established agile, QA and DevOps practices
- Directed product architecture and longer-term technology strategy
- Managed distributed delivery, budgets, security and compliance
- Bridged customer goals, commercial constraints and engineering reality
